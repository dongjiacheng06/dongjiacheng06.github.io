## About Me

I am an undergraduate student majoring in Electrical Engineering at Zhejiang University's [ZJU-UIUC Institute](https://zjui.intl.zju.edu.cn/en). I am fortunate to be advised by [Prof. Yinghao Xu](https://justimyhxu.github.io/). I currently work as a Research Intern at [RobbyAnt](https://www.robbyant.com/). Previously, I worked as a research assistant at the CVNext Lab in the ZJU-UIUC Institute, mentored by [Gaoang Wang](https://cvnext.github.io/).

My research interests lie in vision foundation models and embodied foundation models. I am interested in how visual representations can support understanding of the physical world and guide robots' actions within it. My long-term goal is to build embodied intelligence that connects perception, understanding, and action.
